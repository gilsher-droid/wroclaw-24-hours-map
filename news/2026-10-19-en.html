Autumn news: fountain finale, nature trails and lights

12 to 18 October 2026, checked on 19 October. Future events are labelled as announcements. The accompanying brand image is not a photograph of these events.

Fountain season finale announced for 24 October

The Multimedia Fountain beside Centennial Hall plans its season finale for Saturday 24 October. Two rounds start at 18:00 and 20:00, about 70 minutes each, with free admission. This is an upcoming event. Summer show times should not be used after the season ends; the next season is planned for spring 2027.

Official source and details

New boardwalks at Smocze Uroczysko are ready

The city reported on 15 October that wooden boardwalks and a walking route near Chałupnicza in Swojczyce are complete. The paths separate visitors from wildlife habitats. Stay on marked routes; sightings of wild animals are never guaranteed.

Official source and details

Lumina Park at Zamek Topacz: the season is underway

Update to last week's announcement: the 2026/27 season began on 16 October and is scheduled to run until 14 February. Until 24 October, visits are Wednesday to Sunday, 17:30 to 21:00, with last entry at 20:00. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Cars approach via Tulipanowa; pedestrians use the main gate at Templariuszy 1. Check the operator's changing seasonal hours before travelling.

Official source and details

Ruska works: use marked pedestrian passages

Since 17 October, Ruska between Plac Solny and Kazimierza Wielkiego has been closed to motor vehicles. Pedestrians use marked passages and cyclists follow a diversion. Works are expected to continue into the second quarter of 2027. Follow signs and current city notices.

Official source and details

Bastion Ceglarski: first restoration stage completed

The city reported on 12 October that the first restoration stage, including the artillery terrace, is complete. This is Bastion Ceglarski at Wzgórze Polskie by the river, distinct from Bastion Sakwowy. Completion of this stage does not mean the whole restoration programme is finished; follow local directions during your visit.

Official source and details
