Autumn lights, art and dining

Four useful updates for 5–11 October 2026. Updated 12 October; future events are identified as announcements.

Lumina Park at Zamek Topacz: opening planned for 16 October

The new light season at Zamek Topacz in Ślęza is announced for 16 October 2026; it has not opened as of 12 October. Expect a 1.5 km walking trail with over 200 light installations. Check the operator’s hours and tickets before travelling.

Source and details

Churches of Peace silverwork: on view until 30 December

The National Museum in Wrocław presents silverwork from the Churches of Peace in Jawor and Świdnica from 1 October to 30 December 2026. Admission to this temporary exhibition is free. Pair the museum visit with the historic churches in Lower Silesia; check museum opening hours before visiting.

Source and details

Dyniowe Iluminacje: planned for 13–18 October at the Botanical Garden

The University of Wrocław Botanical Garden schedules Dyniowe Iluminacje for 13–18 October 2026. As of 12 October, this is an upcoming event. Check evening entry hours and ticket conditions with the garden before visiting.

Source and details

RestaurantWeek is underway in Wrocław until 25 November

The autumn RestaurantWeek runs from 7 October to 25 November 2026. Fifty Wrocław restaurants offer three-course menus from 69.99 zł per person plus a booking fee. Choose your restaurant and menu and reserve through the organiser’s website.
