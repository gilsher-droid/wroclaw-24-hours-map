Wrocław this week: a new museum, Christmas plans and easier travel

16 November edition, reviewing 9-15 November 2026. Plan your next visit with dates checked against city and organizer notices. Future events remain announcements.

Tygiel announces its museum opening week

Muzeum Wałbrzyszan Tygiel at Stara Kopalnia plans to open on 16 November, 17:00-20:00. Published opening-week tickets for 16-22 November cost PLN 10. Tuesday-Thursday and Sunday: 10:00-18:00; Friday-Saturday: 11:00-20:00. Some accompanying activities require booking. This is the published programme, not confirmation that the opening has already taken place.

Two Christmas markets plan to open on 20 November

At the main market, visitors are expected from 10:00, followed by a brass-band procession at 16:45 and the opening ceremony at 17:00. The season is announced through 7 January 2027, normally 10:00-21:00, closed 24-25 December and 1 January, with special holiday hours. The separate Nowy Świąteczny Targ on plac Nowy Targ is planned for 20 November-20 December. Check each organizer separately; neither opening is confirmed here.

HU HU HUTA: a Christmas market in an industrial setting

A separate market at Huta Wrocław is announced for 27 November-31 December 2026, with free market entry. Published hours: Monday-Friday 16:00-21:00; Saturday-Sunday 13:00-21:00; closed 24-25 December. Illuminated Christmas trees, food and events are planned. Check show times and special-event conditions with the organizer. The market is still in the future.

Platform numbers should make transfers easier

The city announced on 13 November a six-month pilot from 16 November: 11 stop positions at Galeria Dominikańska and 8 at Plac Jana Pawła II will receive two-digit numbers and orientation maps. Look for the position number as well as the stop name and check current signage and routes. Integration into journey-planning apps is a future goal, not a feature verified across all apps.

Książ announces a Daisy-inspired winter exhibition

The castle’s 9 November announcement schedules Magia Świąt: Daisy. A Christmas Story for 1 December 2026-10 January 2027, with around 200 Christmas trees and decorated castle rooms. The exhibition has not opened yet. Check tickets and visiting hours with the castle; do not assume it is included in every tour.

A new friendship avenue in the park

On 15 November the city reported the opening of Aleja Przyjaźni Ukraińsko-Polskiej in Park Na Niskich Łąkach near Żabia Grobla. The project includes 35 shrubs, a time capsule and a memorial stone. A new local story to discover on a park walk.
