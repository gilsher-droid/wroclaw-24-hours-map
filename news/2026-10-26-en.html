Late October in Wrocław: transport, art and a castle weekend

News from 19–25 October and practical plans for the coming week. The castle events and holiday services are upcoming; the fountain show season has ended.

The fountain show season has ended

The municipal calendar ended the 2026 season on 24 October. Do not plan evening shows using summer times. The fountain stays on the map; the next season is planned for spring 2027, with no exact reopening date announced.

Track works at Łokietka and pl. św. Macieja divert buses 144, 240, 247, 248 and 250. Meanwhile, the tram timetable restores service via Pomorska. Check your current route and departure time before travelling.
