November in Wrocław: Christmas plans and visitor closures

News from 26 October to 1 November, with practical information for your next visit. Christmas markets are still upcoming; check winter closures before setting out.

Main Christmas market: dates announced

Rynek · pl. Solny · Świdnicka · Oławska

Scheduled for 20 November to 7 January 2027, normally 10:00–21:00. Closed 24 and 25 December and 1 January; opens at 13:00 on 26 December. New Year’s Eve has special hours: consult the city notice. The market has not opened yet.

A separate Christmas market at Nowy Targ

Nowy Świąteczny Targ

The organizer and city announce a separate market on plac Nowy Targ, planned for 20 November to 20 December, with crafts, gifts and food. Full daily hours still need checking. This is an upcoming event, distinct from the main market.

Japanese and Botanical Gardens: winter visiting

The Japanese Garden’s winter break runs from 1 November to 31 March. The Botanical Garden’s regular daytime season ended on 31 October. Arranged tours and special activities have separate programmes; this does not mean every evening activity is cancelled. No current Wrocław Botanical Garden light event was verified.

Czocha Castle: closed 5–7 November

Zamek Czocha

The castle announces no entry to the castle, courtyards or grounds on 5–7 November. Visits are announced to resume on 8 November at 11:00. Do not use normal opening hours for the closure dates; check the operator’s notice before travelling.

Official source and details

Książ leads a readers’ poll

Results published on 28 October give Książ 20.46% of votes in the most beautiful Polish castle category. This is a Travelist public poll with the Polish Tourism Organisation, rather than an objective assessment. Check November visiting hours before a trip.

Official source and details

Photography at the Contemporary Museum

Muzeum Współczesne Wrocław · pl. Strzegomski 2A

The museum and co-organizer programme lists a 30 October start for Rzeczywistość symultaniczna. An indoor art option to plan around. The sources disagree about the end date, which we omit. Confirm current hours, tickets and exhibition updates with the museum.
